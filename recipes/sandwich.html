Sandwich

Ingredients

  • Two slices of bread
  • One tomato
  • Any sandwich dressing
  • Butter

Steps

  1. Put the two slices of bread side by side
  2. Cut the tomato into slices
  3. On one of the slices of bread, put the tomato slices
  4. Cover the tomato slices in the dressing
  5. Put the other bread slice on the tomato and the dressing
  6. Head the pan
  7. Spread the butter on it
  8. Put the sandwich on the pan
  9. Wait till it gets a crunchy crust
  10. Serve and enjoy!

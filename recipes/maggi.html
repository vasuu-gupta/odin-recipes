Maggi

Ingredients

  • One packet of maggi
  • 200ml of water
  • Pot

Steps

  1. Put the 200ml of water in the pot
  2. Put the pot to boil
  3. Open the Maggi packet
  4. Seperate the contents of the packet
  5. Once the water starts boiling, put the noodles in the pot
  6. Once the noodles soften up, put the seasonings in the pot
  7. Wait till noodles become yellow
  8. Serve and enjoy!
